Emergency — India

Do not wait for a summary if there is chest pain, severe breathlessness, sudden face or arm weakness, heavy bleeding or loss of consciousness.

Call 108 (ambulance) or 112 (national emergency).

How VoiceCare works

VoiceCare listens inside the app. Tap the mic, speak in your own language, and AI on VoiceCare's own server writes it down. Gemini AI then organises it into the eight-section summary your doctor reads.

  • Never diagnoses illness or prescribes medicine.
  • Never invents symptoms or history you did not mention.
  • Keeps the AI key on the server, never in the app.
