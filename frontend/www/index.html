How VoiceCare protects you

VoiceCare listens inside the app. Tap the mic, speak in your own language, and our secure server writes it down over HTTPS without saving audio to disk. Gemini AI organises it into an 8-section summary in English.

  • Never diagnoses illness or prescribes medicine.
  • Never invents symptoms or history you did not mention.
  • Keeps the AI key on the server, never in the app.
